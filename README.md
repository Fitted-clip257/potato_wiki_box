## ❓ Common Questions

**Does the software upload my files?**
No. Every process happens on your computer. Your files remain on your drives.

**How do I delete my data?**
You delete your workspace by deleting the folder where the software stores your notes. The application does not hide data in system directories. You clean your workspace by deleting the generated Markdown files.

**Can I import files from other apps?**
Yes. If your files exist as text or Markdown, the application reads them. Move your existing folders into the workspace directory. The tool creates new links based on the content of those files.

**Does this require a subscription?**
No. This tool is free. You own the software and your results.

**What happens if I turn off my computer?**
The software saves your progress as you work. All changes are written to the disk immediately. You find your information exactly as you left it when you open the app again.

**Where does the software save my index?**
The software saves the knowledge base inside the folder you select at startup. You move this folder to a different drive or computer at any time.

**How do I fix errors?**
Restart the application if the screen freezes. Check that your folder permissions allow the app to write new files. Most errors come from read-only folders. Ensure your workspace folder is not marked as read-only.
